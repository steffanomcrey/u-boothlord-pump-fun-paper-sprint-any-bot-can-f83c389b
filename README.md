First-Minute Flow Sniper: a pump.fun paper-trading bot for the make.fun replay harness. It buys only coins whose opening tape already looks like a winner's (70+ SOL of buys from 50+ distinct buyers, up 50%+ by 30 seconds after creation), rides them with a trailing stop and no fixed take-profit, and exits on a 15% stop, a creator sell after entry, or a 10-minute hold. A tiny 0.02 SOL probe lane tops the coin count up to the 20-coin guardrail in every 6-hour window. Local dev grade: +0.73 SOL net, +0.46 SOL at 2x fees, max drawdown 0.05.
